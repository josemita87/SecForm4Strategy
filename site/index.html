How to read this page. The model gives each filing a probability that the stock falls by more than 5% in 30 days (a short winner). Walk-forward folds choose the hyper-parameters and the probability cutoff. The model is then scored once on a time-ordered holdout: the latest filings, which the selection never saw. Every number below comes from that holdout.

Training facts

Holdout metrics

PredictorAPROC-AUCMedian short returnShorts

AP is the average precision of the ranking of short winners; the base rate baseline predicts one constant probability and scores the holdout's share of short winners. Ticker history predicts the ticker's past share of short winners. Shorts are the rows at or above the probability cutoff. A short return is the negated actual return, so a positive value is a profit.
